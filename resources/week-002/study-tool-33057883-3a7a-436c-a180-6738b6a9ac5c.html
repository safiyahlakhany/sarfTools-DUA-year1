ṣarf Study — Week 2: المُضارِع Scales

Negative form · 6 scales of ثلاثي مجرد · translation both directions

Review — forming the مُضارِع

  • A verb is ماضي (past), مضارع (present/future), or أمر (command).
  • To make a verb مضارع, add a prefix — أتين, the علامات المضارع.
  • The prefix gives person & gender; the suffix gives number.
Third person male / third person plural femaleيَ Second person (M/F) or third person femaleتَ First person, singular, M/Fأَ First person, dual/plural, M/Fنَ
Dualان Plural maleون Singular femaleين Plural Feminineنَ

Negative form — مَنْفي

Add لَا before the مضارع verb.

يَنْصُرُ → لَا يَنْصُرُ

(The past tense is negated with مَا.)

Verb scales

  • صرف كبير — the 14 verbs showing every combination of POV, gender, and number.
  • صرف صغير — the set scale showing how a verb is built from its root letters.

The 6 scales of ثلاثي مجرد

Each باب is defined by the vowel on the ع الكلمة. Both the past and present ع-vowels are needed to fix the باب.

#بابPast ع ماضيPresent ع مضارعMeaning
1نَصَرَ – يَنْصُرُfatḥa (ـَ)ḍamma (ـُ)to help
2ضَرَبَ – يَضْرِبُfatḥa (ـَ)kasra (ـِ)to strike
3سَمِعَ – يَسْمَعُkasra (ـِ)fatḥa (ـَ)to hear
4فَتَحَ – يَفْتَحُfatḥa (ـَ)fatḥa (ـَ)to open
5كَرُمَ – يَكْرُمُḍamma (ـُ)ḍamma (ـُ)to be noble
6حَسِبَ – يَحْسِبُkasra (ـِ)kasra (ـِ)to reckon

Determining the باب — Hans Wehr

A Hans Wehr entry gives the vowels as letters — u = ḍamma, i = kasra, a = fatḥa. Both the past and present vowels are listed; you need both, since باب 3 and باب 4 share the same present vowel.
