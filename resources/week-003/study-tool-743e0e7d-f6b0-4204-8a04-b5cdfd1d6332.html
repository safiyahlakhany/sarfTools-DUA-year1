Making the passive — مجهول

To turn a مضارع معروف into مضارع مجهول:

  • Give the first letter (the علامة المضارع) a ḍamma.
  • Give the ع الكلمة a fatḥa.

يَنْصُرُ → يُنْصَرُ  (he helps → he is helped)

This shift is the same across all six abwab — the passive vowel pattern doesn't depend on the باب.

Effect on meaning

  • The verb becomes passive: “he is helping” → “he is being helped”.
  • The نائب الفاعل takes the place of the فاعل.

Transitive & intransitive

accepts an object (مفعول)متعدّي does not accept an objectلازم

Only transitive (متعدي) verbs can be made passive (فعل مجهول).

Hans Wehr — transitivity markings

  • ه — accusative of a person
  • ﻫ — accusative of a thing
  • ها — feminine of animate beings
  • هم — a group of persons
  • If a definition includes “(smne)” or “(smth)”, the verb is متعدي.
