الماضي — مثبت ومنفي

Past tense, positive and negative — lesson, then four practice sections

Lesson: الماضي — مثبت and منفي

Review

  • فعل متعدي — accepts an object. Example: نصر (to help). Can be made into مجهول.
  • فعل لازم — does not accept an object. Example: جلس (to sit). Cannot be made into مجهول.

What we've covered so far

Present tense (مضارع) scales: مثبت معروف · مثبت مجهول · منفي معروف · منفي مجهول.

Roadmap

  • Today: past tense pronouns (معروف مثبت ومنفي).
  • Next week: مجهول.

Key notes

  • There are no prefixes in past tense verbs.
  • The suffixes can be singular or compound!

Suffixes — singular

— (no suffix)singular masculine third personضَرَبَ
تْsingular feminine third personضَرَبَتْ
تَsingular masculine second personضَرَبْتَ
تِsingular feminine second personضَرَبْتِ
تُsingular masculine / feminine first personضَرَبْتُ

Suffixes — plural

واplural masculine third personضَرَبُوا
نَplural feminine third personضَرَبْنَ
تُمْplural masculine second personضَرَبْتُمْ
تُنَّplural feminine second personضَرَبْتُنَّ
نَاplural masculine / feminine first personضَرَبْنَا

Suffixes — dual

اdual masculine third personضَرَبَا
ا + تdual feminine third person (compound)ضَرَبَتَا
ا + تُمْdual masculine / feminine second person (compound)ضَرَبْتُمَا
